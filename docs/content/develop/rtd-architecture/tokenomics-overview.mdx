The RTD source fork defines the native `Coin<rtd::rtd::RTD>` type. In
`crates/rtd-framework/packages/rtd-framework/sources/rtd.move`, one RTD is
represented by one billion MIST, and the source-level total supply constant is
ten billion RTD. These constants describe the code used for genesis creation;
they do not establish that a public RTD network has launched or that any tokens
are available for sale.

The fork also contains validator staking and storage-fund Move modules inherited
from the Sui architecture. Their actual parameters and initial balances depend
on a specific RTD genesis and protocol configuration. For the protocol design,
see [Consensus](/develop/rtd-architecture/consensus) and [Storage on Rtd](/develop/rtd-architecture/rtd-storage).

## Public economic policy

No verified RTD public genesis allocation, investor vesting schedule, airdrop
policy, exchange listing, circulating supply, or market price is established by
this repository. The original [Sui economics paper](/paper/sui-tokenomics.pdf)
is retained as upstream research with its original authorship; it is not an RTD
economic whitepaper.

Before publishing any RTD tokenomics claims, the network operator must approve
the genesis distribution and reconcile every supply, staking, subsidy, and
storage-fund statement against that genesis and deployed protocol. Until then,
use this page only as a guide to source-level mechanics, not as financial or
network-launch information.

## Storage fund

The fork retains storage-fund mechanics in its system packages. The amount
funded at genesis and the effective rebate parameters must be read from the
specific RTD network configuration; the inherited Sui figures are not RTD
policy.

## Understanding the voting power formula

Validator voting power depends on the stake recorded by the deployed system
state and its protocol configuration. Verify it against the actual RTD network
state before using it for governance or economic calculations.
