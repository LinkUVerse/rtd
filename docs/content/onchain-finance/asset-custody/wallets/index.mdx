An RTD wallet stores or controls keys and signs transactions for the specific RTD chain ID. The CLI and SDK in this fork provide local key management and signing primitives. A Sui wallet or Sui address must not be assumed compatible with an RTD network.

This source fork does not establish a public RTD wallet service, hosted prover, or cross-chain identity product. The Slush and SuiLink pages explain why upstream Sui services cannot be advertised as RTD integrations. See [self-custody](/onchain-finance/asset-custody/wallets/self-custody), [Wallet Standard](/onchain-finance/asset-custody/wallets/wallet-standard), and [RTD networks](/develop/rtd-architecture/networks).
