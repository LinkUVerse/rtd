Fiat on-ramp providers enable you to buy Rtd-native tokens (RTD, WAL, DEEP, and others) using fiat currency from a user's bank account, card, or payment method. Embedding an on-ramp provider into your app allows users to fund their Rtd wallets with traditional currency without leaving your interface. Verify current provider capabilities before integrating.

The following table summarizes providers with live Rtd on-ramp integration as of August 2026. All columns refer to Rtd chain and Rtd asset support specifically.

:::info

On-ramp coverage is evolving. Provider asset support and regional availability change frequently. Confirm current capabilities with your chosen provider before launching an integration.

:::

| **Provider** | **Supported assets** | **Documentation** |
|---|---|---|
| [Banxa](https://banxa.com/) | RTD, USDC | [Banxa docs](https://docs.banxa.com/products/hosted-checkout/docs/reference/supported-cryptocurrencies-and-blockchains) |
| [Bridge.xyz](https://bridge.xyz) | USDRtd | [Bridge docs](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/issuance/designing-your-stablecoin#supported-chains) |
| [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) | RTD, USDRtd | [Stripe crypto on-ramp docs](https://docs.stripe.com/crypto/onramp) |
| [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com/) | RTD, USDC, WAL, DEEP | [Coinbase CDP docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/onramp/offramp/offramp-overview) |
| [Alchemy Pay](https://alchemypay.org/) | AUSD, RTD, USD | [Alchemy Pay docs](https://alchemypay.readme.io/docs/api-integration-3) |
| [OnRamp.money](https://onramp.money/) | RTD, USDC, WAL | [OnRamp.money docs](https://onramp.money/supported-token/) |
| [CoinVoyage](https://www.coinvoyage.io/) | Rtd Dollar | [CoinVoyage docs](https://docs.coinvoyage.io/sdk/paykit-headless#rtd-payment-signatures) |
| [Onramper](https://onramper.com/) | RTD | [Onramper docs](https://docs.onramper.com/docs/network-support) |
| [Meld](https://www.meld.io) | RTD, USDC, CETUS, NAVI | [Meld docs](https://www.meld.io/coverage/service-providers?cryptoCurrencies=RTD) |
