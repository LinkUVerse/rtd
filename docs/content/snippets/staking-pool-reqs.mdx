### Validator staking pool requirements

RTD has no public Mainnet or public validator onboarding program. A local RTD
deployment gets its validator set, stake, and epoch settings from its genesis
configuration. Inspect those values and the active protocol configuration for
the network you operate before accepting validator or staking requests.

[Upstream Sui SIP-39](https://github.com/sui-foundation/sips/blob/main/sips/sip-39.md)
describes a Sui voting-power proposal. Its thresholds, rollout schedule, and
onboarding process are not an RTD policy.
